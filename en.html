Frequently asked questions

Didn't find your question? Write to us: beinke3@gmail.com

Does BlitzTV include channels or movies?

No. BlitzTV is a player only and contains no content. You need a playlist from an IPTV provider. Please only use playlists you are entitled to use.

Which playlists work?

Xtream Codes logins (server, username, password) and M3U playlists as a link or file. An Xtream M3U link is set up as an Xtream login automatically – with guide, series and catch-up.

How much does BlitzTV cost?

You can try BlitzTV for 7 days for free, with all features. After that it's a one-time purchase – no subscription, all future updates included.

Can I use several playlists?

Yes. Add as many playlists as you like and switch between them at any time. Favorites and progress are kept per playlist.

Is there an Xbox version?

An Xbox version is in the works. The plan: buy once, use it on PC and Xbox.

What do I need?

A PC with Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit) and an internet connection. For 4K a fast connection is recommended.
